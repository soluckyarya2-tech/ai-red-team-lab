# Introduction to Home Security Systems

Home security has evolved significantly over the past decade. Modern systems
combine physical locks, electronic sensors, and networked monitoring into
unified platforms accessible from mobile devices.

Traditional deadbolts remain the foundation of physical security. A quality
grade-1 deadbolt, properly installed with 3-inch screws reaching the wall
stud, resists forced entry far better than electronic alternatives.

Electronic systems add layers: door/window sensors detect entry, motion
detectors cover interior zones, and cameras provide verification. The most
effective deployments combine all three tiers with professional monitoring.
